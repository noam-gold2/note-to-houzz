Here's why this one is personal.

I grew up in the home of a construction project manager and site supervisor, later a developer abroad. I watched the work up close, and I saw exactly where it got stuck: payments, change orders, who owed what to whom, and no clear picture of any of it.

Years later, as a consultant, I built the thing I'd watched them need. For a firm building luxury homes, projects worth ₪5–30M with dozens of vendors each, I built a system that tracked every payment request from vendor to developer. Clear statuses, and a dashboard showing what was paid, pending, and outstanding, down to each vendor. It worked well enough that we replicated it across ~15 more clients.

That's Houzz Pro, more or less. Built by hand, because the pros I knew had no better option. So when I read this role, owning the document journey from estimate to paid invoice, tracking how change orders hit margins, I'm not learning the problem. I've lived it from both sides: the contractor's frustration and the builder's tool.
